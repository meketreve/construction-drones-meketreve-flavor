Version: 1.9.0
Date: 2026-09-24
  Features:
    - Garages close enough to hear each other share what their chests hold, so a chest wired to one garage also
      supplies work around another. The drone still walks, so a chest further from the job than a garage can
      hear is left alone.
    - Holding a garage on the cursor now shows the area of the garages already built, to place the new one by.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.8.0
Date: 2026-09-24
  Features:
    - Garages now work as one. A drone reports to whichever garage is closest to where it finished, not to the
      one that sent it out, and a garage with work but no drones borrows one from the closest garage that has
      any, up to 96 tiles away. Drones end up where the work is without being handed out one garage at a time.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Version: 1.5.0
Date: 2026-09-24
